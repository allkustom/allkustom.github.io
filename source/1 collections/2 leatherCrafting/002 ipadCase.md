# Building Height with Leathers

To store the iPad safely, I needed walls with sufficient thickness. However, shaping a single leather sheet into walls relied too heavily on the material’s own thickness and made it difficult to achieve a **crisp, angular form**.

<br>

As a solution, I **stacked several leather layers** to build up the walls. While not the most efficient approach, it provided consistent thickness for impact cushioning and successfully matched the intended form.
