Looking back at my journey of overcoming **my color weakness**, I realized that I needed to prepare for my expedition into Terra incognita(Unknown Land) by going to my roots. Going back to the concept of **Purusha, shoes are regarded as unclean, untrained instincts**. By taking off your shoes, you can step into sacred land.
This artwork creates an embodiment of my past self through black and white shoes, and **by taking them off**, I've started a new artistic journey.
