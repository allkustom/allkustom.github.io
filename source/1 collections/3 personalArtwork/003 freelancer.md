### Participated in Freelancer, a pilot/test film, as both an actor and a props fabricator.
As an actor, I dressed up as a futuristic assassin with formal suit and armor.
As a props fabricator, I built a functional laser sight and mounted it to a prop pistol. The laser sight was 3D printed and wrapped in electrical tape to match the film’s aesthetic.
