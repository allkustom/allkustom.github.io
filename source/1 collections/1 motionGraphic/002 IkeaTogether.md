# Why I Chose IKEA

To practice seamless transition motion graphics, I needed a subject that met several requirements. First, the objects and brand system had to be modular and recombinable. Second, I needed easy access to real-world references. Third, the brand had to communicate a clear, recognizable message. IKEA was the most suitable choice because it satisfied all of these conditions.
</div>

  ## 'Together'
Divided into 2 sections.
**Section 1**, uses seamless transitions to highlight IKEA’s brand value of enabling flexible interior concepts through modular furniture combinations.
**Section 2**, follows a couple building a shared space through IKEA furniture.

<br>

Rather than simply showcasing the products, focuses on the user narrative made possible by the brand’s value.

# Character Rigging
Used **'Duik Bassel' and 'Joysticks & Sliders'**. To achieve smooth joint motion, I added circular overlap shapes at the intersections to prevent off-shaping during rotation. However, because the character was designed in simliar tones, same-color overlaps reduced clearity. Added an outline that appears only when layers overlap, ensuring clear separation when parts intersect.
</div>
