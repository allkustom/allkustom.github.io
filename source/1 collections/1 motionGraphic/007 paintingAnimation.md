## Client Request
The client wanted the narrative and context within each painting to be conveyed through motion. To achieve this, I researched the background and meaning of each work and designed animations that **reflected its story and setting.**

<br>

To animate the characters, I first separated the subjects from the background. I then split the characters into **joint-based segments and used mesh deformation** to create rich, expressive motion while keeping the rotation pivots fixed.
