[Raum Art Center](https://www.theraum.co.kr/)(Click Link) is a wedding and social venue designed like an old medieval castle. Its **marble columns** are treated as a signature element that represents both the building and the brand logo. I needed to create a transition-style logo animation that **reinforced the brand identity** while remaining flexible for use across different videos. I used the marble column pattern to build a full-screen transition and applied sound effects to maintain the venue’s classic and refined tone.
