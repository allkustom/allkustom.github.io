[Raum Art Center](https://www.theraum.co.kr/)(Click Link)  hosts a matinee concert on the last Tuesday of every month in a performance hall defined by brick arch structures on the 4th floor of the main building. Because the concert always took place in the same venue, it maintained a consistent classic atmosphere, but it lacked opportunities for new visual approaches. The company shifted its operational strategy by installing a beam projector and aimed to integrate projection mapping into the matinee convert. To operate the system effectively, I self-studied **MadMapper** and performed on-site calibration tailored to the space and conditions to achieve accurate mapping. I also developed content that fit the arched architecture while aligning with the concert program. In addition, used the pre-show waiting period **to introduce performers and promote partner brands and products.**
